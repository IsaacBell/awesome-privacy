## Browsers

- [Firefox](https://www.mozilla.org/firefox/) - Open-source browser with strong tracking protection and container tabs.
- [Brave](https://brave.com/) - Chromium-based browser with aggressive ad and tracker blocking by default.
- [Safari](https://www.apple.com/safari/) - Apple's browser with Intelligent Tracking Prevention; Apple ecosystem only.
- [Tor Browser](https://www.torproject.org/) - Browser built for anonymity through the Tor network.
- [LibreWolf](https://librewolf.net/) - Firefox fork with privacy and security hardening by default.
- [DuckDuckGo Browser](https://duckduckgo.com/app) - Mobile browser with built-in tracker blocking and search.

## Search Engines

- [DuckDuckGo](https://duckduckgo.com/) - Independent search engine that does not track users.
- [Startpage](https://www.startpage.com/) - Google results served through a privacy proxy.
- [Kagi](https://kagi.com/) - Paid, ad-free, tracking-free search engine.
- [Mojeek](https://www.mojeek.com/) - Independent crawler and search index with no tracking.
- [SearXNG](https://docs.searxng.org/) - Self-hostable metasearch engine that aggregates results without tracking.

## Secure Messaging

- [Signal](https://signal.org/) - Open-source, end-to-end encrypted messaging, voice, and video calls.
- [WhatsApp](https://www.whatsapp.com/) - End-to-end encrypted messaging; owned by Meta with extensive metadata collection.
- [iMessage](https://support.apple.com/messages) - End-to-end encrypted messaging for Apple devices.
- [Session](https://getsession.org/) - Anonymous messaging app without phone numbers.
- [Matrix](https://matrix.org/) - Open standard for decentralized, end-to-end-encrypted real-time communication.
- [Element](https://element.io/) - Flagship client for the Matrix protocol on web, mobile, and desktop.
- [Briar](https://briarproject.org/) - Peer-to-peer encrypted messaging that routes through Tor and Bluetooth.
- [Threema](https://threema.ch/en/) - Paid encrypted messenger that works without a phone number or email address.

## Password Managers

- [Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com/) - Open-source password manager with free personal use.
- [Bitwarden Browser Extension](https://bitwarden.com/download/) - Autofill and vault access for Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari.
- [1Password](https://1password.com/) - Cross-platform password manager with family and team plans.
- [Apple Passwords](https://support.apple.com/passwords) - Built-in password manager for Apple devices.
- [KeePassXC](https://keepassxc.org/) - Offline, open-source password manager.
- [Proton Pass](https://proton.me/pass) - Encrypted password manager from the Proton suite.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

- [YubiKey](https://www.yubico.com/) - Hardware security key for phishing-resistant authentication.
- [Authy](https://authy.com/) - Cross-platform two-factor authentication app.
- [Google Authenticator](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1066447) - Time-based one-time password authenticator.
- [Aegis](https://getaegis.app/) - Open-source 2FA app with encrypted backups.
- [Ente Auth](https://ente.io/auth) - Open-source 2FA authenticator with end-to-end encrypted backups.
- [2FA Directory](https://2fa.directory/) - Community-maintained list of which sites support two-factor authentication.

## VPNs

- [Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/) - Privacy-focused VPN that accepts anonymous payments.
- [Proton VPN](https://protonvpn.com/) - VPN from the makers of Proton Mail with a no-logs policy.
- [IVPN](https://www.ivpn.net/) - Audited no-logs VPN with anti-tracking features.
- [OVPN](https://www.ovpn.com/) - Swedish no-logs VPN with built-in ad and tracker blocking.
- [Windscribe](https://windscribe.com/) - VPN with a generous free tier and configurable firewall rules.
- [Private Internet Access](https://www.privateinternetaccess.com/) - Large no-logs VPN network with open-source clients.
- [NordVPN](https://nordvpn.com/) - Wide VPN network with specialty servers and Threat Protection features.

## DNS

- [Cloudflare 1.1.1.1](https://one.one.one.one/) - Fast, privacy-focused DNS resolver.
- [Quad9](https://www.quad9.net/) - DNS resolver that blocks known malicious domains without logging queries.
- [NextDNS](https://nextdns.io/) - Configurable DNS firewall with blocking and analytics.
- [AdGuard DNS](https://adguard-dns.com/) - DNS-level filtering that blocks ads and trackers across all devices.
- [Pi-hole](https://pi-hole.net/) - Self-hosted, network-wide ad and tracker blocker for your home network.

## Ad and Tracker Blockers

- [uBlock Origin](https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock) - Efficient wide-spectrum content blocker for browsers.
- [Privacy Badger](https://privacybadger.org/) - EFF tracker blocker that learns as you browse.
- [AdGuard](https://adguard.com/) - Ad, tracker, and malware blocker for browsers, mobile, and desktop.
- [Ghostery](https://ghostery.com/) - Privacy extension that blocks trackers and reveals who is tracking you.

## Backup and Cloud Storage

- [Backblaze](https://www.backblaze.com/) - Unlimited cloud backup for one computer.
- [iCloud+](https://www.apple.com/icloud/) - Apple's cloud storage and backup service.
- [Google One](https://one.google.com/) - Cloud storage and backup for Google accounts.
- [Proton Drive](https://proton.me/drive) - End-to-end encrypted cloud storage.
- [Tresorit](https://tresorit.com/) - End-to-end encrypted cloud storage for teams and individuals.
- [Immich](https://immich.app/) - Self-hosted photo and video backup with face recognition and sharing.
- [Sync.com](https://www.sync.com/) - Zero-knowledge encrypted cloud storage with file sharing.
- [Filen](https://filen.io/) - End-to-end encrypted cloud storage with affordable lifetime plans.
- [Cryptomator](https://cryptomator.org/) - Client-side encryption for files before uploading them to any cloud provider.

## Email Services

- [Proton Mail](https://proton.me/mail) - End-to-end encrypted email service.
- [Tutanota](https://tutanota.com/) - Encrypted email and calendar service.
- [Forward Email](https://forwardemail.net/) - Open-source, self-hostable email forwarding service.
- [Canary Mail](https://canarymail.io/) - Privacy-focused email client with end-to-end encryption options.
- [StartMail](https://www.startmail.com/) - Encrypted email service with disposable aliases, from the makers of Startpage.
- [Mailbox.org](https://mailbox.org/en/) - Privacy-friendly email, calendar, and cloud storage hosted in Germany.
- [Mailfence](https://mailfence.com/) - Encrypted email with built-in documents, calendar, and contacts.
- [Proton Unlimited](https://proton.me/pricing) - All Proton services in one plan: Mail, VPN, Drive, Calendar, and Pass.

### Email Aliasing

- [SimpleLogin](https://simplelogin.io/) - Email alias service for hiding your real address.
- [Addy.io](https://addy.io/) - Open-source email alias service for creating unlimited aliases.
- [Firefox Relay](https://relay.firefox.com/) - Mozilla's email and phone number masking service.

## Identity and Account Security

- [Have I Been Pwned](https://haveibeenpwned.com/) - Check if an email address has appeared in data breaches.
- [IdentityTheft.gov](https://www.identitytheft.gov/) - U.S. government resource for reporting and recovering from identity theft.
- [AnnualCreditReport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com/) - Free annual credit reports from U.S. bureaus.

## Data Removal and Tracking Control

- [DeleteMe](https://joindeleteme.com/) - Service that finds and removes your data from data broker sites.
- [Incogni](https://incogni.com/) - Automated data broker removal service from the makers of Surfshark.
- [Google Takeout](https://takeout.google.com/) - Download and export all data Google stores about you.
- [JustDeleteMe](https://justdeleteme.xyz/) - Directory of direct links for deleting or deactivating online accounts.

## Operating Systems

- [GrapheneOS](https://grapheneos.org/) - Hardened, privacy-focused Android-based OS for Pixel phones.
- [CalyxOS](https://calyxos.org/) - Privacy-focused Android distribution with microG and sandboxed Google Play.
- [Qubes OS](https://qubes-os.org/) - Desktop OS that isolates tasks into secure virtual machines.
- [Linux](https://www.linux.org/) - Open-source operating system family with many privacy-friendly distributions.

## Metadata and File Privacy

- [ExifTool](https://exiftool.org/) - Command-line tool for reading, writing, and stripping metadata from files.
- [MAT2](https://0xacab.org/jvoisin/mat2) - Metadata anonymization toolkit that cleans files before sharing.
- [ExifCleaner](https://exifcleaner.com/) - Desktop app for batch-removing metadata from photos and documents.

## Transparency and Leak-Test Tools

- [BrowserLeaks](https://browserleaks.com/) - Test suite that reveals what your browser leaks about you.
- [Privacy Tests](https://privacytests.org/) - Independent browser privacy test results and comparisons.
- [whoer.net](https://whoer.net/) - Check your IP, proxy, VPN, and anonymity status.
- [DNS Leak Test](https://dnsleaktest.com/) - Detect whether your DNS queries leak outside your VPN tunnel.
- [Terms of Service; Didn't Read](https://tosdr.org/) - Crowdsourced ratings of website terms of service and privacy policies.

## Books

- [Extreme Privacy: What It Takes to Disappear](https://www.amazon.com/Extreme-Privacy-What-Takes-Disappear/dp/B0DCJN61GF) - Michael Bazzell's step-by-step guide to removing your personal data from public sources.
- [The Malicious Use of Artificial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.07228) - Foundational 2018 report on how AI could be misused and the security implications.
- [Crypto Anarchy, Cyberstates, and Pirate Utopias](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262621519/crypto-anarchy-cyberstates-and-pirate-utopias/) - MIT Press essays on cryptography, privacy, and alternative online governance.
- [The Transparent Society](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/david-brin/the-transparent-society/9780465027903/) - David Brin's classic argument for reciprocal transparency over one-way surveillance.
- [Data Grab: The New Colonialism of Big Tech](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/D/bo216184200.html) - How large tech companies extract personal data and what to do about it.
- [CCRU Writings 1997–2003](https://www.urbanomic.com/book/ccru-writings-1997-2003/) - Collected writings of the Cybernetic Culture Research Unit on cyberculture and hyperstition.
